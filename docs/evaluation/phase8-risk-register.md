# Phase 8 evaluation risk register (v1.1)

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / control | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Author-generated labels bias results | High | High | Disclosure; consistency checks (evidence spans verified through the application's parsers; labels precede model runs); worksheet for independent human review prepared; no AI second pass presented as review | Open (no reviewer assigned) |
| R2 | Small held-out split gives wide intervals (test 75 queries, about 40 answerable) | High | Medium | Wilson/bootstrap intervals; classification wording that never reads overlap as equivalence; challenge split evaluated once after test | Open |
| R3 | 1.5B model cannot reach the quality targets | High | Medium | Targets stay NOT MET if missed; grounded-answer coverage reported beside citation correctness so refusal cannot hide failure | Open |
| R4 | Overfitting to dev | Medium | High | Group-disjoint splits; selection rule fixed before experiments; test/challenge sealed and hashed | Controlled |
| R5 | Test exposure through debugging | Medium | High | Runner refuses held-out splits without an explicit flag; disclosure rule; no second final run | Controlled |
| R6 | Optimisation weakens a safety control | Low | Critical | Safety gates 0-tolerance; full security, FI and UAT re-run at the end; any regression fails the phase | Controlled |
| R7 | Template-style synthetic text inflates scores | Medium | Medium | Several surface forms per fact, hard negatives, conflicting and superseded records, similar identifiers; near-duplicate check across splits (max Jaccard < 0.9); limitation disclosed | Open |
| R8 | Latency growth from added stages | Medium | Medium | Overhead recorded per experiment; trade-off tables; rollback if p95 budget exceeded without a quality benefit | Open |
| R9 | Measurement noise on Windows/CPU (background load, thermal throttling) | Medium | Low | Repetitions, warm-up separated from timing, resource sampling, background load noted | Open |
| R10 | Specification text unavailable, catalogue completeness unverified | Certain | Medium | Completeness reported UNVERIFIED; reconciliation framework ready; catalogue not edited | Open (needs the specification file) |
| R11 | Retrieval ranking non-determinism (approximate HNSW, tie ordering) | Medium | Low | Repeat pass measures it; post-ingestion ANALYZE for stable plans; ties reported | Measured in baseline |
| R12 | Model hallucination of numeric signals feeding deterministic priority rules | Observed in smoke run | High | Measured as hallucinated-numeric-signal rate; candidate mitigation (value must occur in the report text) in the experiment plan, with safety review | Open |
| R13 | Mock external systems differ from real systems (inventory returns 404 for synthetic parts) | Certain | Low | Enrichment outcomes reported as such; workflow claims limited to the mocks | Accepted |
| R14 | Dataset or labels regenerated by accident after freeze | Low | High | Hash manifest, frozen split hashes, byte-identical regeneration test, `--verify` command | Controlled |
