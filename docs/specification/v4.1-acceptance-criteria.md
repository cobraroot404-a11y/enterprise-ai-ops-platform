# V4.1 acceptance criteria

Acceptance criteria that the original specification states or implies, with the observed status. The proposed targets of Part 21 are **targets, not achieved results** (the specification says so). Thresholds added by later approved instructions are listed separately and never replace an original target.

## Original proposed targets (Part 21)

| ID | Original target | Status | Evidence / observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| V41-21.03 | * Recall@5: at least 85%. | Verified | Observed met on dev (88.5%) and held-out baseline (89.5%); the interval overlaps the target, so attainment is not statistically established |
| V41-21.04 | * Citation correctness: at least 95%. | Partial | Dev failure taxonomy: of 29 non-ok answerable dev queries only 2 are retrieval losses; 17 are answers withheld by the verifier (quotation or number mismatch) and 10 are wrong citation or an incomplete answer. Experiment D1 (answer multi-part questions part by part) was REJECTED (coverage 64.4% vs 66.7%, citation correctness 86.4% vs 91.9%). Targets remain NOT MET; the likely lever is a stronger local model (needs owner approval to download) and independent confirmation data |
| V41-21.05 | * Structured ticket schema validity: 100%. | Partial | Held-out raw model validity was 18 of 20 (NOT MET at the model level). A development experiment (30 new reports) found the model invents an asset id for 16 of 16 reports naming none; the application now identifies the asset itself (30 of 30 correct on the development cases) and records the model's value, so the model-level metric will no longer count it; confirmation needs fresh independent data |
| V41-21.06 | * Unauthorized cross-tenant access: zero successful attempts in the defined test suite. | Verified | Met on the evidence shown |
| V41-21.07 | * Unauthorized ticket submissions: zero. | Verified | Met on the evidence shown |
| V41-21.08 | * Duplicate tickets during tested retries: zero. | Verified | Met on the evidence shown |
| V41-21.09 | * Critical UAT pass rate: 100%. | Verified | Met on the evidence shown |
| V41-21.10 | * Transient failure recovery: at least 95% in the defined test suite. | Verified | Suite defined by the author (11 transient-failure cases against mock ticketing, plus two controls); 11 of 11 recovered; coarse, same-author, mock external system |
